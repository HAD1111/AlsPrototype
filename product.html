18K solid gold retains its glow through generations with simple mindfulness:

  • Avoid direct contact with perfumes, chlorine, and abrasive chemicals.
  • Store each piece individually in its ALMASA pouch to prevent contact scratching.
  • Clean periodically with warm water, mild soap, and a soft-bristled brush.
  • Annual ultrasonic inspection is complimentary at our flagship atelier.
