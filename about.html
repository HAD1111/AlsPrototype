THE FINE JEWELRY HOUSE

Jewelry with a sense of permanence.

We believe fine jewelry should never be rushed or disposable. Shaped by Mediterranean heritage, our atelier exists to forge 18K gold creations that witness life’s most indelible chapters.

“We shape gold so that memories may outlive the seasons.”
A SENSE OF PERMANENCE

Created for the chapters you choose to mark.

ALMASA was established with a singular conviction: fine jewelry should feel deeply intimate, enduringly noble, and visually serene. In an era dominated by transient fashion cycles and hollow mass-market jewelry, we remain devoted to the slow, exacting discipline of Mediterranean haute joaillerie.

Our workshop draws inspiration from the enduring light of the Levant and the ancient trade routes of Phoenician and Roman goldsmiths—where jewelry was worn as an armor of dignity, a testament to affection, and an heirloom passed through maternal lineages.

Every piece that bears the ALMASA hallmark is conceived to outlive fleeting aesthetics, retaining its structural majesty and emotional warmth across lifetimes.

THE NOBLE STANDARD

Why 18K Solid Gold is Non-Negotiable

18-karat gold—known as 750 purity—is the international gold standard of high jewelry houses. Comprising exactly 75% pure gold alloyed with copper and silver, it strikes the ideal alchemy: the deep, rich buttery warmth of fine gold united with the structural durability required for everyday wear.

Unlike 14K gold, which can appear pale and rigid, or 24K gold, which is too soft to securely hold diamond settings or retain articulated links, 18K gold develops a soft, living patina that enriches with time. It does not tarnish, does not degrade, and is completely hypoallergenic against the most sensitive skin.

THE BENCH DISCIPLINE

Executed by hand, calibrated to microns.

Before a stone is set or a collar articulated, our bench jewelers hand-finish every invisible contour. We believe comfort is as crucial as visual splendor: an heirloom that sits awkwardly in a jewelry drawer is a failed creation.

Our serpent tubogas ribbing is tensioned with internal gold memory springs that adapt naturally to body movement. Our diamond pavé settings are microscopically aligned under high-power stereo loupes to eliminate catch points on delicate silk garments. The result is fine jewelry that feels weightless, fluid, and second-skin smooth.

THE UNBOXING RITUAL

Bespoke Packaging & Concierge Gifting

The reception of fine jewelry is an indelible ritual. Every ALMASA piece arrives encased in our signature midnight-teal velvet jewel box, lined in cream suede and tied with warm ivory grosgrain ribbon.

Included with every delivery is an embossed certificate specifying total gold weight, hallmark registration, and care guidance, alongside a personalized calligraphy card for gifting. Whether destined for an anniversary, a proposal, or personal milestone, the presentation conveys the timeless gravity of what lies within.
