OUR HERITAGE

Made to be part of your story.

At ALMASA, jewelry is never conceived as ephemeral ornamentation. Shaped by Mediterranean heritage and the warmth of ancient goldsmith traditions, each creation is sculpted in noble 18K solid gold to accompany life’s most intimate milestones.

From private triumphs and anniversary vows to quiet moments of personal grace, our pieces are imbued with meaning—crafted with structural integrity and ergonomic comfort so they can be worn effortlessly today, and passed down as cherished family heirlooms tomorrow.

ATELIER STANDARDS

The Art of Fine Craftsmanship

Every curve, joint, and prong is executed with meticulous care to honor the dignity of 18K gold.

Mastery at the Bench

True luxury reveals itself in the unseen: the hand-polished inner bevels that glide across the skin, the balanced heft of solid gold rather than hollow impressions, and the microscopic precision of diamond settings aligned to capture light from every angle.

Our artisans combine age-old Mediterranean goldsmithing with modern calibrated stone setting, ensuring each creation retains its beauty and structural resilience through generations of daily wear.

01

750 Gold Purity

We work exclusively in 18K solid gold—an ideal alloy of 75% pure gold, precisely balanced with noble alloys to achieve the signature warm champagne hue and enduring tensile strength.

02

Ergonomic Comfort

Jewelry must feel as divine against the skin as it looks in the mirror. Every ring shank and bangle is contoured with comfort-fit curves to ensure effortless all-day wear.

03

Heirloom Longevity

Our pieces undergo rigorous tension tests and clasp calibration before leaving the atelier, accompanied by complimentary lifetime cleaning and inspection guidance.
